HOW FATELORE WORKS

Fatelore is designed for character creation, particularly during Session 0.

At the beginning of a game, draw up to three cards and use them as sparks to help shape your character.

Rather than starting with a blank character sheet, Fatelore gives you something to start with.

A spark.

THE THREE LENSES

Every Fatelore card comes from one of three lenses.

They are not rules.
They are simply different ways of looking at a character.

NATURE

What were you born with?

The things that were yours before you had any say in them.

A gift. A flaw. A bloodline. A secret. Something waiting inside you.

NURTURE

What shaped you?

The people, places and experiences that made you who you became.

What you were taught. What you witnessed. What you survived.

WILL

What did you choose?

The moments when you decided for yourself.

The risks you took. The things you pursued. The things you rejected. The things you sacrificed.

“Don’t tell me who my character is.
Ignite my imagination.”

Every card is created with one of the three lenses in mind.

But the category isn’t an answer.

A Nature card might inspire something about your character’s upbringing.

A Nurture card might become a defining choice.

A Will card might reveal something they were born with.

There is no correct interpretation.

The card is the spark.
The story is yours.

WHY FATELORE?

FOR NEW PLAYERS

Creating a character from a blank sheet can be daunting.

Fatelore gives new players sparks of inspiration they can use to discover who their character might be.

FOR EXPERIENCED PLAYERS

You’ve already explored countless characters, histories and motivations.

Fatelore introduces unexpected ideas that can trigger something new.

IT ISN’T A CHARACTER GENERATOR

Fatelore doesn’t create your character.

It doesn’t give you a backstory.

It doesn’t tell you what your character must be.

It gives your imagination something to burn with.

The card is the spark.
The story is yours.
