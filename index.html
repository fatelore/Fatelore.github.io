How Fatelore Works

Fatelore is designed to be used during character creation, particularly during Session 0. It gives players something to react to instead of asking them to create a character from a blank page.

01

DRAW

Draw a card. You can draw one, two or three. There is no required combination.

02

INTERPRET

The card doesn't give you an answer. Decide what the image and words mean for your character.

03

CREATE

Follow the idea wherever it takes you. Keep it. Change it. Reject it. Let it lead somewhere unexpected.

The Three Lenses

Every Fatelore card begins with one of three ideas. They are lenses, not rules.

NATURE

What were you born with?

The things that were yours before you had any say in them. A gift. A flaw. A bloodline. A secret. Something waiting inside you.

NURTURE

What shaped you?

The people, places and circumstances that made you who you became. What you were taught. What you witnessed. What you survived.

WILL

What did you choose?

The moments when you decided for yourself. The risks you took. The things you pursued, rejected or sacrificed.

"Don't tell me who my character is. Give me an idea."

Every card is created with one of the three lenses in mind, but the category is not an answer.

A Nurture card might make you think about something your character was born with. A Nature card might inspire a choice they made. A Will card might reveal something about the person who raised them.

There is no correct interpretation.

Why Fatelore?

For new players

Starting with a blank character sheet can be daunting. Fatelore gives new players creative sparks they can use to discover who their character might be.

For experienced players

Experienced players have already explored many of their own ideas. Fatelore introduces unexpected concepts that can push those ideas somewhere new.

It's not a character generator

Fatelore doesn't create your character for you. It doesn't give you a backstory or tell you what your character must be.

It gives you something to react to.
