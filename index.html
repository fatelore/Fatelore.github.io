How Fatelore Works

Fatelore is designed for character creation, particularly during Session 0.

Instead of staring at a blank character sheet, you begin with a spark.

01

DRAW

Draw a card. You can draw one, two or three. There is no required combination.

02

IMAGINE

Look at the image. Read the provocation. Let it ignite an idea.

03

CREATE

Follow the idea wherever it leads. Keep it. Change it. Or let it spark something completely different.

The Three Lenses

Every Fatelore card begins with one of three ideas.
They are lenses, not rules.

NATURE

What were you born with?

The things that were yours before you had any say in them. A gift. A flaw. A bloodline. A secret. Something waiting inside you.

NURTURE

What shaped you?

The people, places and experiences that made you who you became. What you were taught. What you witnessed. What you survived.

WILL

What did you choose?

The moments when you decided for yourself. The risks you took. The things you pursued, rejected or sacrificed.

"Don't tell me who my character is.
Give me an idea."

Every card is created with one of the three lenses in mind. But the category isn't an answer.

A Nature card might inspire something about your character's upbringing. A Nurture card might become a defining choice. A Will card might reveal something they were born with.

There is no correct interpretation.

The card is the spark.
The story is yours.

Why Fatelore?

For new players

Creating a character from a blank sheet can be daunting. Fatelore gives new players sparks of inspiration they can use to discover who their character might be.

For experienced players

You've already explored countless characters, histories and motivations. Fatelore introduces unexpected ideas that can ignite something new.

It isn't a character generator

Fatelore doesn't create your character. It doesn't give you a backstory. It doesn't tell you what your character must be.

It gives your imagination something to burn with.
