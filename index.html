An RPG Character Creation Deck

FATELORE

A deck of provocations.

Fatelore doesn't tell you who your character is. It gives you something to think about.

How Fatelore Works

01

DRAW

Draw a card during character creation. You might find something familiar. Or something completely unexpected.

02

INTERPRET

The card doesn't give you an answer. Decide what it means to you and your character.

03

CREATE

Follow the idea wherever it takes you. One card might be enough. Three might change everything.

"Don't tell me who my character is.
Give me an idea."

Fatelore is designed to help new players find a starting point and experienced players discover ideas they might never have thought of themselves.

The cards aren't instructions. They aren't character templates. They are sparks for your imagination.

Try Fatelore

Enter your name if you'd like, then draw up to three cards. There is no right way to interpret them.


Remember

The card doesn't tell you what it means. That's your decision.
